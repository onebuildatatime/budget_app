Budget Buddy Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

Budget Buddy is local-first

Your budget data stays on your device by default. No account required to use the app.

What stays on device

By default, all budget data stays locally on your device:

  • Monthly limits
  • Categories and subcategories
  • Transactions and expenses
  • Goals
  • Theme, language, and currency preferences
  • Bank account labels

Local-only storage

Budget Buddy does not require an account and does not upload your budget to a cloud service. Calculations, charts, and summaries are created on your device.

You can export a copy using the app's data tools. An export leaves the app only when you choose a destination through the system share sheet.

Data deletion

You can delete all your data anytime:

  1. Open the app and go to Settings
  2. Export a copy first if you want to keep one
  3. Delete budgets and transactions you no longer want, or remove the app to erase its local data

What is NOT sold or shared

Budget Buddy does not:

  • Sell your budget data
  • Share your data with advertisers
  • Use your data for marketing
  • Require an account or data sharing to use the app
